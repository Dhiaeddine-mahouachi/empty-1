IDENTITY

Make it feel like your brand

Choose your avatar, background image or color and profile details. Keep the visual style consistent with the way you present yourself elsewhere.

LINKS

Your most useful destinations

Bring your website, menu, social profiles and contact destinations together. Give each link a clear title so visitors know where it leads.

LOCATION

Help people find you

Include a map link for your venue, shop or event. Visitors can open the destination directly from your profile.

INTERACTION

Small games, extra engagement

Add the available Snake or Tetris mini games to your profile. Visitors can play on their phone using the on-screen controls.

SHARING

A link and QR for your profile

Use your activated profile link online or share its QR code on printed materials. For an NFC card, ask AuraDigital about card setup.

MANAGEMENT

Your profile dashboard

Sign in to find your profile, review its status and manage its content. Activation remains subject to payment verification and approval.
