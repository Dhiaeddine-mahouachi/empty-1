What is an AuraPop?

An AuraPop is an interactive profile that brings your links, menu, location and optional mini games together. Visitors open it using a link or QR code.

Do visitors need an app or account?

No. Visitors open an activated public profile in their browser. You use your own account to manage your profile.

Can I preview my profile?

Yes. Use the builder to choose your content and visual style, then review the preview before submitting your request.

When is my profile available publicly?

After payment verification and approval by AuraDigital. A submitted request or builder preview does not mean the profile is activated.

What does it cost?

The homepage shows plan previews. Final payment and activation details are confirmed by AuraDigital. Check the details for your request before paying.

Can I update my links and background?

Use your account dashboard to manage your profile’s content and appearance. If you cannot access a profile, contact AuraDigital with your request details.

Can I use it for my restaurant?

Yes. Add links to your menu, social channels and location. AuraMenu is the separate product for building a full digital restaurant menu.

Can I share it on an NFC card?

Your live profile link can be used for NFC sharing. Contact AuraDigital to arrange a card and its setup.

Where do I log in or get help?

Use Sign in in the navigation to open your account. For payment, activation or account help, contact AuraDigital.

Need help choosing the right setup?
Contact AuraDigital with your idea, your content and the way you want to share it.
